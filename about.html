01 / MY STORY

THE STORY
BEHIND IT.

Motorsport has always been more than just what happens on track. Behind every lap, every overtake and every podium there are engineers, mechanics, journalists, strategists, drivers and countless people working together.

Studying engineering made me especially interested in that side of racing. I wanted to understand the details that are often hidden behind the final result.

That is where The Paddock Journal comes in. It is my own space to explore motorsport from a different perspective and to tell the stories that happen beyond the finish line.
