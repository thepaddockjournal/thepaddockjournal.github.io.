My Story

From engineering to the paddock

Motorsport has always been more than just what happens during a race. The cars, the engineering, the strategy, the people and the atmosphere of a paddock are all part of the story.

As someone studying engineering, I became increasingly interested in understanding what happens behind the scenes — how a car is developed, how teams make decisions and how thousands of small details come together to create a racing weekend.

The Paddock Journal is my way of exploring that world. It is a personal project, but also a place to learn, research and share the things about motorsport that genuinely interest me.
